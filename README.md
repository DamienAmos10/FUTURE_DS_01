# FUTURE_DS_01

## Task 1 – Business Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

This project focuses on building an interactive **Business Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard** to analyze online retail sales data and generate meaningful business insights. The dashboard provides a clear view of revenue trends, customer activity, product performance, and country-wise sales distribution to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🔧 Tools Used

* **Power BI** – For creating interactive dashboards and visualizations  
* **Excel** – For initial data exploration and preprocessing  
* **CSV Dataset** – Online Retail Sales Dataset  

---

## 📊 Key Features

* Overview of total revenue, orders, customers, and quantity sold (KPIs)  
* Monthly sales trend analysis  
* Top-performing products by revenue  
* Country-wise revenue analysis using map visualization  
* Average order value analysis  
* Interactive slicers for month and country filtering  

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* The United Kingdom contributes the highest share of total revenue  
* Sales peak strongly during November and December  
* Home décor and gift products are top-selling categories  
* International markets like Germany, France, and Netherlands show growth potential  
* Dashboard visuals help identify seasonal demand and expansion opportunities
